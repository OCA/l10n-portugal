Odoo country list was mapped to the InvoiceXpress names as documented at
https://invoicexpress.com/api-v2/documentation/appendix

The InvoiceXpress country list carries legacy and alternate names.
Most modern names are also accepted (e.g. both "Timor-Leste" and
"East Timor"), so Odoo names are used where InvoiceXpress lists them.

These InvoiceXpress country names have no Odoo counterpart:

Burma (Myanmar is used instead)
Canton and Enderbury Islands
Dronning Maud Land
East Timor (Timor-Leste is used instead)
International Monetary Fund
Johnston Island
Kampuchea (Cambodia is used instead)
Midway Islands
Netherlands Antilles (dissolved; Odoo uses successor codes
Bonaire/Curaçao/Aruba/Sint Maarten - verify against InvoiceXpress
if invoicing to these territories)
Samoa (America) (American Samoa is used instead)
Tahiti (French Polynesia is used instead)
Upper Volta (Burkina Faso is used instead)
Wake Island
Western Samoa (Samoa (Western) is used instead)
Zaïre (Congo, Democratic Republic is used instead)
