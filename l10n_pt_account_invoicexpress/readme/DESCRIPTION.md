Issue Portuguese customer invoices, invoice receipts, simplified
invoices, and credit notes through
[InvoiceXpress](https://invoicexpress.com), a certified invoicing
service. InvoiceXpress issues and numbers the legal document when you
post it in Odoo.

Odoo uses the certified InvoiceXpress PDF for printing and standard
Send & Print. Sending directly from InvoiceXpress is optional and is
not triggered by posting.
