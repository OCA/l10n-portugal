Adds a configurable VAT adjustment legal basis catalog and lets users
select an applicable basis on Portuguese customer and vendor credit
notes. It captures information used for VAT adjustment reporting (boxes
40 and 41), but does not generate the annexes.

Installation
============

**Fresh installation**

Install this addon normally. It does not require ``l10n_pt_vat`` or
``l10n_pt_account_vat``. The standard legal bases are loaded
automatically. You can add or customize entries as described in Usage.

**Upgrading an existing database**

If you need to keep legacy adjustment norms, install this addon after
updating ``l10n_pt_vat`` and before uninstalling it. The install hook
matches standard norms by XML ID, preserves legacy values, and copies
custom norms and their credit-note links. If there is no legacy data,
the standard catalog is available without migration.

Usage
=====

VAT adjustment legal bases are maintained at *Accounting/Invoicing >
Configuration > Accounting > VAT Adjustment Legal Basis*. Each entry has
a name, an optional note shown on the credit note form, and two flags
telling whether it applies to customer credit notes, vendor credit
notes, or both.

On a draft customer or vendor credit note of a Portuguese company, the
"VAT Adjustment Legal Basis" field next to the journal lets the
bookkeeper pick an applicable norm; its note is displayed below the
field. The selector only appears for Portuguese credit notes and is
locked once the document is posted.
