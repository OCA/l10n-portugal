VAT adjustment legal bases are maintained at
*Accounting/Invoicing > Configuration > Accounting > VAT Adjustment
Legal Basis*. Each entry has a name, an optional note shown on the
credit note form, and two flags telling whether it applies to customer
credit notes, vendor credit notes, or both.

On a draft customer or vendor credit note of a Portuguese company, the
"VAT Adjustment Legal Basis" field next to the journal lets the
bookkeeper pick an applicable norm; its note is displayed below the
field. The selector only appears for Portuguese credit notes and is
locked once the document is posted.
