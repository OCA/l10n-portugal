1. In Time Off, set up the public holidays on your Portuguese work calendars.
2. Create and approve Annual Leave allocations for eligible employees. Check each employee's entitlement before allocating days. The module does not grant days automatically.
3. Review the default approval settings for Annual Leave in Time Off configuration.
4. Use the most specific Portuguese absence type when recording an absence. The default types do not determine whether an absence is paid or automatically prepare a report.
5. In Time Off > Reporting > Vacation Schedule, choose the year, then print the current Portuguese company's approved annual leave periods. Review and post the schedule at the workplace.
