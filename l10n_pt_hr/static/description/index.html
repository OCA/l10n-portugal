Portugal - Human Resources

Portuguese localization support for Human Resources.

Features:

  • Demo data for the “PT Company” (base.demo_company_pt from l10n_pt): a full set of Portuguese employees mirroring the core hr demo data, including a Portuguese department tree, work location, working schedule, contract versions and department managers, with Portuguese job titles.
  • Extra translations (i18n_extra/pt.po) for the core demo Job Positions used by the demo data.

Important

This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning. Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production. More details on development status

Terms marked new have no dedicated field or translation in Odoo CE or EE today. Terms marked exists already have a pt translation in the core hr, hr_holidays, hr_attendance or EE hr_payroll modules.

Notes on proposed terms:

  • Categoria profissional is the term used on Portuguese payslips (recibo de vencimento) and in collective agreements. English: “professional category” (“job category” is also acceptable).
  • Escalão de remuneração (“pay grade”) is official for independent workers, whose contributions are computed on a “remuneração convencional” fixed in one of the pay grades (escalões) referenced to the IAS. It does not apply to employees on a standard contract (conta de outrem), whose contributions are a percentage of actual pay. Keep it only if the module targets independent workers.
  • Regime de Segurança Social is the official term (seg-social.pt): e.g. “regime geral dos trabalhadores por conta de outrem”, “regime dos trabalhadores independentes”. English: “Social Security scheme”.
  • Escalão de IRS is official (OE tables, gov.pt): the IRS is divided into escalões (brackets). English: “IRS bracket” or “tax bracket”. For payroll withholding, the operative table is the “tabela de retenção na fonte” (withholding table) — model the brackets behind it.
  • Nível de Habilitações Académicas: prefer Nível de qualificação, the official term of the Quadro Nacional de Qualificações (QNQ, levels 1-8, aligned with EQF/ISCED). English: “qualification level”. “Habilitações” is colloquial. Odoo CE ships hr.employee.certificate (“Certificate Level”) translated as “Escolaridade” — European Portuguese users will expect the QNQ level.
  • Seguro de Acidentes de Trabalho: official term for the mandatory work accident insurance; commonly abbreviated SAT. English: “work accident insurance” or “occupational accident insurance”.

Statutory reporting requirements

Portugal has no single annual “income declaration” from employer to worker anymore. The recurring obligations are two monthly declarations, both due by day 10 of the following month (Portaria n.º 33/2024 as amended by Portaria n.º 289/2025 for the AT; the SS declaration follows the same calendar):

  • DMR-AT — Declaração Mensal de Remunerações to the Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira (AT): employment income (category A), IRS withholding, mandatory social protection contributions, union dues.
  • DMR-SS — Declaração de Remunerações to Segurança Social, I.P.: remuneration per worker per month split by nature code.

Related obligations: payment of withheld IRS (day 20), payment of SS contributions (days 1-25), the annual Relatório Único to DGCP (former GEP, roughly March-April), and the payslip content required by art. 276.º of the Código do Trabalho.

Employee master data required by the DMR-AT

The DMR-AT quadro 5 (“Relação dos titulares dos rendimentos”) is keyed by the worker’s NIF, with one line per income type and region of obtention:

Rendimentos do ano / anos anteriores Payroll results per income code, split by year and region of obtention
Tipo de rendimento (código) Income categorization on salary rules (e.g. A68 IRS Jovem, exempt codes)
Local onde foram obtidos Region only — C Continente, RA Açores, RM Madeira, E Estrangeiro (Portaria n.º 274/2014 instructions; art. 17.º n.º 3 CIRS: where the work is performed). Derived from the work location address, not the establishment

The withholding itself depends on employee attributes declared to the AT (not on the DMR): marital status / couple situation (tabela: não casado, casado único titular, casado dois titulares), number of dependents, disability, fiscal residence (Continente, Açores, Madeira), and IRS Jovem eligibility (age, qualification level per QNQ, years of income).

Employee master data required by the DMR-SS

The DMR-SS file has fixed-length records of 118 characters, model RC4008 (normas de preenchimento, seg-social.pt). The structure is:

  • R0 — file header (model + version), once per file.
  • R1 — entity header, one per estabelecimento × taxa contributiva.
  • R2 — remuneration lines per worker per month of reference.
  • R3 — totals closing each R1 block.

“Deverão existir tantos registos (R1) quantos os diferentes estabelecimentos e diferentes taxas que a Entidade Empregadora tenha a declarar” — the OCC practical guide (June 2025) states the same in plain terms: workers of each estabelecimento go in separate declarations, and one declaration per contribution rate. So the grouping key is (estabelecimento, taxa contributiva), not the worker alone. Each R1 carries NISSEE (SS number of the employing entity), ESTABEE (4-char establishment code registered in Segurança Social Direta — SS rejects unknown/closed codes, error DS31), NIF, RAZSOC, DATREM; each R3 carries the same keys plus TAXA and TOTREGS. A single file may contain several R1–R3 blocks.

Each R2 line carries:

Field Master data needed
NISS Social security number
Nome do trabalhador Full name
Data de nascimento Date of birth
Mês de referência Payroll period
Dias de trabalho Worked days (one decimal, may be negative)
Natureza da remuneração (código) Remuneration nature per line: P base, F subsídio de férias, N subsídio de Natal, R subsídio de refeição, S trabalho suplementar, T trabalho noturno, M subsídios regulares mensais, X subsídios regulares não mensais, C comissões, B/O prémios mensais/não mensais, A ajudas de custo e transportes, D compensação por cessação, 2 férias pagas não gozadas, 6 diferenças de vencimento, I contrato intermitente

Estabelecimento (ESTABEE) SS establishment code of the employing entity — via the worker’s establishment (l10n.pt.establishment)
Taxa contributiva Contribution rate per worker (differs for MOE, independent workers, exemptions); also the second dimension of the R1/R3 grouping

Master data checklist for both declarations

  • NIF (employee contact VAT), full name, date of birth, NISS — worker identification
  • Marital status, number of dependents, disability, fiscal residence — IRS withholding table selection
  • IBAN — salary payment and refund account
  • Professional category — required on the recibo de vencimento (art. 276.º CT) and used in SS contexts
  • Contract type, schedule — payslip; work location resolves the DMR-AT region and the DMR-SS/RU establishment
  • Social Security scheme (regime) — determines the contribution rate and the applicable rules
  • Union membership — quotizações sindicais
  • Income categorization per salary rule — maps payroll lines to the DMR-AT income codes and DMR-SS nature codes

Annual Relatório Único (RU) — DGCP

The Relatório Único (Portaria n.º 55/2010) is the annual social-activity report to the DGCP (former GEP), delivered through the relatoriounico.pt application, typically in a March-April window. It has a Rosto (employer and establishments), Anexo A (Quadro de Pessoal — staff as of 31 October), Anexo B (entries/exits during the year), Anexo C (continuous training), Anexo D (occupational health and safety), Anexo E (strikes) and Anexo F (service providers). Estagiários (interns) are included in the headcount and the Anexo A.

Establishments (“Unidades Locais”) in the RU

The modelo RU (Portaria n.º 55/2010; modelo oficial MODELO_RU.pdf, delivered via the SGURI platform at relatoriounico.pt) has a Section II: Identificação da(s) Unidade(s) Local(is) (Estabelecimentos) — one block per physical establishment including the sede. Each block needs:

RU field Data needed
Número da unidade local Assigned by SGURI in the platform (SUL — Sistema de Unidades Locais); not master data
Nome / datas de início e fim l10n.pt.establishment.name, activity period
Localização e contactos Address down to freguesia + phone/email — via address_id
Actividade económica principal (CAE) CAE Rev. 4 code — mandatory since the 2026 delivery (year 2025)
Situação perante a actividade, motivo e período Status + reason + dates
Total de pessoas ao serviço (31 de Outubro) Headcount snapshot per establishment
Número de trabalhadores por conta de outrem TCO count per establishment

Every anexo is keyed by unidade local: Anexo A’s header carries NIF + NISS + número da unidade local, and each worker row has a “Nº da unidade local (estabelecimento)” field. Anexos B–F are likewise reported per UL (Anexo D even asks for SST technician counts per UL). This makes the establishment — not the department — the statutory grouping unit of the RU, and the 31-October reference date means the worker→establishment assignment must be reconstructible historically (hr.version).

Proposed fields

Concrete proposal for every element marked new or partial above. Naming follows the l10n_pt_ prefix used by sibling modules. All personal data fields sit in the private-information group (hr.group_hr_user); contract-scoped fields use hr.group_hr_manager where the core equivalent does.

Placement rules

  • hr.version holds the employee’s personal data in Odoo 19 (ssnid, marital, children, private address fields all live there). Fields that describe the worker or vary per contract version go there.
  • hr.employee holds data that must survive contract changes: the first contractual admission date and the last promotion date.
  • Per-year statutory tables (IRS brackets, IAS, minimum existence) get dedicated models; they are data, not employee fields.
  • Open-ended statutory lists get one dedicated model each (l10n.pt.collective.contract, l10n.pt.professional.category, l10n.pt.union, l10n.pt.profession, l10n.pt.pay.grade) rather than one generic attribute mechanism or fields.Selection. Closed code tables stay fields.Selection. The module is self-contained: it does not depend on hr_employee_master_data.
  • Salary-rule mappings (income category, DMR nature codes, contribution base) extend hr.salary.rule and therefore land in l10n_pt_payroll, not this module.
  • Rejected: a separate l10n_pt.employee model — it would duplicate hr.version and break the core versioning UI.

ssnid validation (NISS): implemented as a constraint on hr.version.ssnid, applied only to employees of Portuguese companies (country_id = PT) so other countries keep their own SSN conventions. The NISS has 11 digits. The first digit identifies the holder type (1 = national, 2 = foreign natural person — employees always start with one of these). The last digit is a check digit computed over the first 10 digits with prime weights 29, 23, 19, 17, 13, 11, 7, 5, 3, 2: check = 9 − (Σ dᵢ·pᵢ mod 10). Demo data uses mathematically valid numbers. This validates format only — whether a NISS is actually assigned is confirmed only by Segurança Social.

identification_id validation: the document number is stored in the core hr.version.identification_id field. For employees of Portuguese companies it is normalized (uppercase, no spaces) and checked against the selected document type; with no type selected, PT employees are treated as other. identification_id of other countries is neither normalized nor validated.

Cartão de Cidadão: the document number is 12 characters, format DDDDDDDD C VV D — 8-digit Número de Identificação Civil (NIC), its check digit, a 2-character card version (A–Z, 0–9), and a final check digit. Two independent checks:

  1. NIC check digit (9th char): mod-11 with weights 9..2 over the 8 digits — the same rule as the old BI and the NIF. check = resto < 2 ? 0 : 11 − resto.
  2. Final check digit (12th char): Luhn variant in base 36 over all 12 chars, right to left, doubling every second character (A=10 … Z=35, subtract 9 when the doubled value ≥ 10); the sum must be a multiple of 10.

Bilhete de Identidade (BI): the legacy document number is 9 digits — the same 8-digit NIC (zero-padded) plus the same mod-11 check digit as the CC’s 9th character.

Residence permit and other document numbers have no Portuguese check-digit scheme — format validation only (A–Z, 0–9). Passports are not part of l10n_pt_id_doc_type: they use the core hr.version.passport_id / passport_expiration_date fields and are not validated here.

Note on work accident insurance (SAT): l10n.pt.work.insurance is a policy record (insurer, policy number, coverage period) that employees point at — several employees can share one policy, which fits the common case of an employer policy covering the whole staff. For employees on a standard contract the coverage is the employer’s obligation; the per-worker link matters for independent workers (trabalhadores independentes), who must hold their own SAT policy. Whether the employer’s master policy also belongs on res.company is an open question below.

Note on professional categories: each IRCT defines its own category table, so l10n.pt.professional.category carries an optional irct_id. The employee form filters the category list to the selected IRCT plus generic categories (no IRCT).

Note on IRS withholding table selection: l10n_pt_irs_table_id is a stored computed field implemented in l10n_pt_payroll — the employee never picks a table directly. The annual Despacho (e.g. n.º 1179/2026, Circular 3/2026) numbers the dependent-work tables I–VII by exactly three determinants, all declared on the employee: family situation (l10n_pt_irs_family_status), the number of IRS dependents (l10n_pt_irs_dependents — a dedicated count, since IRS dependents can differ from dependent children), and disability of 60% or more (l10n_pt_disability_degree). Tables IV–VII are the disabled variants of I–III. Caveats: these are the mainland (continente) tables — the Açores and Madeira have their own Despacho table sets with the same numbering but different values, which l10n_pt_payroll must select by region; non-resident taxpayers pay a flat rate and pensioners use tables VIII–XI, both out of scope here; IRS Jovem is a reduction computed by payroll (age, QNQ level, years of income), not a table determinant.

l10n.pt.establishment — estabelecimento / unidade local

Decided: a dedicated PT model, not fields on hr.work.location. The establishment is a company-level legal concept — it is the grouping unit of the DMR-SS file and of every RU anexo, its SS code is registered in Segurança Social Direta against the employing entity, and it is needed by non-HR flows (payroll, accounting, future SAF-T/IES obligations). hr.work.location stays the HR-level “where the worker physically is” record.

The same record serves both authorities, but the two identifiers are independent: Segurança Social calls it the estabelecimento (ESTABEE, a code registered in SS Direta), the RU calls it the unidade local (SGURI assigns the número). They are stored as separate fields and are never inferred from each other; values are plain text so leading zeros survive. If a real-world mismatch ever appears (SS grouping ≠ physical site), separate records remain possible.

The statutory lists use dedicated l10n.pt.* models instead of a generic attribute mechanism: the lists differ enough in shape (CPP has ~2 700 coded entries, professional categories are scoped to an IRCT, IRCTs have BTE references) that one model per list stays simpler than a generic attribute/value pair. The fields are stored on hr.version, so values are contract-scoped and versioned like the rest of the employment data. Both payroll-facing lists are coded models so l10n_pt_payroll can map each code to its rates: l10n.pt.ss.regime.code → the regime’s contribution rates, l10n.pt.irs.withholding.table.code → the year’s withholding brackets. A generic effective-dated attribute mechanism remains available in the hr_employee_master_data module in the OCA/hr repository for attributes that do not fit these dedicated lists; l10n_pt_hr does not depend on it.

The hr.pt.irs.* and hr.pt.fiscal.year models belong to l10n_pt_payroll (they only matter once salary rules exist), but are listed here because they consume the employee fields above. Data, not fields

Some catalog elements need records rather than fields. These belong in data files, not models:

  • Leave types: hr.leave.type records for Férias, Baixa médica, Licença parental (PT-PT labels).
  • Work entry types and salary rules for Horas suplementares, Subsídio de alimentação, Subsídio de transporte — defined by l10n_pt_payroll.
  • QNQ and RU code tables — Selection values and demo data.
  • CPP table — l10n.pt.profession rows, loaded from the official CPP/2010 classification (large table, import tooling needed).
  • IRCT and per-IRCT professional category tables — l10n.pt.collective.contract and l10n.pt.professional.category rows per collective agreement.
  • Escalões de remuneração (TI pay grades referenced to IAS) — l10n.pt.pay.grade rows, only if independent workers are supported.

HR Employee Master Data (hr_employee_master_data, OCA/hr)

The hr_employee_master_data module (OCA/hr) owns the whole attribute mechanism: attribute definitions, allowed values, and effective-dated per-employee assignments with full change history. l10n_pt_hr seeds the Portuguese attributes as data and reads the current values; it does not own the mechanism.

Models

Model Purpose Key fields
hr.employee.attribute A named list of allowed values for one HR data element name, code (unique technical key), employee_field_id (optional link to an hr.employee field), default_country_ids, required_country_ids, active
hr.employee.attribute.value One allowed value of an attribute attribute_id, name, code, reference (record mirrored for many2one-linked attributes), sequence, active, company_id
hr.employee.attribute.company Per-company activation of an attribute company_id, attribute_id, is_required
hr.employee.history One effective-dated assignment of an attribute value to an employee — the history record employee_id, attribute_id (related, stored), value_id, date_start, date_end, reason_id, is_current
hr.employee.change Transient wizard behind “Employee Change” employee_id, date_start, reason_id, lines of attribute_id + value_id
hr.employee.change.reason Coded change reason code, name, attribute_ids

Attribute scope — countries and companies

Attributes are not global-on; they are scoped:

  • hr.employee.attribute.default_country_ids — countries for which the attribute is a default (shown/applied unless the company turns it off). required_country_ids — countries for which it is required. The employee’s country (company country by default) selects which attributes apply.
  • hr.employee.attribute.company — each company keeps its own list of active attributes, and for each one whether it is required or optional for that company. This is the per-company override of the attribute’s country defaults.

Change reasons

reason on assignments and on the Employee Change wizard is a coded hr.employee.change.reason record (code, name), not free text. Each reason optionally lists the hr.employee.attributes it applies to — empty means the reason applies to any attribute (e.g. “Correction” fits everything, “Promotion” may only fit professional_category / pay_grade / irct).

Assignment semantics

  • An assignment is valid from date_start until the next assignment’s date_start for the same employee and attribute (date_end computed, like hr.version). is_current means the period covers today.
  • At most one current assignment per (employee, attribute) — partial unique constraint, same pattern as hr.version’s (employee_id, date_version) index.
  • Writing a new assignment closes the previous current period (date_end = new date_start); overlaps are rejected.
  • reason_id is a coded hr.employee.change.reason (see above); the reason’s attribute_ids constrains which attributes it may be used with (empty = any).
  • History is reconstructible for any date, not just today — this is what the RU 31-October snapshot and the monthly DMR-SS grouping consume.

Employee form — Master Data page

A “Master Data” notebook page on hr.employee lists the employee’s master data as one line per attribute:

Column Source
Attribute hr.employee.attribute.name
Current value current assignment’s value_id
Start date current assignment’s date_start
Change reason current assignment’s reason

Only attributes with at least one assignment appear — an employee with no union does not get an empty union line.

Row actions:

  • History — opens the assignment list for that employee + attribute, all periods ordered by date_start.
  • Change — opens the Employee Change wizard prefilled with that attribute (single-line change).

Page action:

  • Employee Change — opens the wizard empty, for bulk changes.

Employee Change (bulk)

The hr.employee.change wizard takes one date_start and one reason_id, for the whole change, plus a list of (attribute, new value) lines — the reason’s attribute_ids must cover every attribute in the change. Confirming creates all assignments atomically and closes each previous current period. Typical use: a promotion that changes professional category, pay grade and IRCT on the same date in one recorded change.

A single-attribute change is the same wizard with one line — there is no separate single-change model.

Relationship to l10n_pt_hr

  • l10n_pt_hr is self-contained: the PT lists are the dedicated l10n.pt.* models above, and the fields (l10n_pt_irct_id, l10n_pt_professional_category_id, l10n_pt_union_id, l10n_pt_profession_id, l10n_pt_pay_grade_id) are stored on hr.version, so they are contract-scoped and versioned with the rest of the employment data. There is no dependency on hr_employee_master_data.
  • Coded list models (l10n.pt.ss.regime, l10n.pt.irs.withholding.table) feed payroll: each record’s code is the stable key l10n_pt_payroll maps to contribution rates and withholding brackets per fiscal year.
  • hr_employee_master_data remains available in the OCA/hr repository as an optional generic mechanism (effective-dated attribute assignments with change history) for HR data elements that do not fit the dedicated PT models; installing it does not change how l10n_pt_hr stores data.

Open questions for hr_employee_master_data

  • Whether assignments should also link to hr.version for attributes that are genuinely contract-scoped (e.g. a different IRCT per contract version) — v1 assumes employee-level only.
  • Whether the Master Data page shows attributes with no assignments — the per-company hr.employee.attribute.company list decides which attributes are active at all; whether inactive-but-assigned or never-assigned attributes still appear needs a UX decision.
  • Access rights: read for hr.group_hr_user, write for hr.group_hr_manager — same split as the private-info fields.

Usage

Demo employees

When the database is created with demo data enabled, the module adds a full set of employees to the “PT Company” demo company. Switch to that company and open Employees to see them.

Load testing

To create large numbers of employees for load testing, use the odoo populate command, which duplicates the existing demo employees:

$ odoo populate -d <database> --models hr.employee --factors 100

Known issues / Roadmap

Status: draft

Decisions

  • The module catalogues the HR data elements required for Portugal, with the Portuguese term and the recommended English term for each. The catalog lives in CONFIGURE.md.
  • Terminology follows official Portuguese sources: seg-social.pt for Social Security regimes and contribution brackets, OE/gov.pt for IRS brackets and withholding tables, QNQ/DGERT for qualification levels.
  • Where Odoo CE/EE already ship a pt translation, it is reused; where the shipped term is a Brazilian variant (holerite, auxílio-transporte, Licença, Escolaridade), the European Portuguese term is recommended instead.
  • Rejected: “Nível de Habilitações Académicas” — colloquial; the official term is “Nível de qualificação” (QNQ).
  • Rejected: “Escalão de Remuneração” for employees — it applies to independent workers (trabalhadores independentes) on conventional remuneration; standard employees contribute on actual pay.
  • The recurring statutory declarations are the monthly DMR-AT (AT) and DMR-SS (Segurança Social), both due by day 10 of the following month. Their required employee master data is catalogued in CONFIGURE.md.
  • New fields are placed per the rules in CONFIGURE.md “Proposed fields”: worker and contract-scoped data on hr.version, career-history data on hr.employee, per-year statutory tables in dedicated models, and salary-rule mappings in l10n_pt_payroll.
    • Rejected: a separate l10n_pt.employee model — duplicates hr.version and breaks the core versioning UI.
  • Open-ended statutory value lists get one dedicated model each (l10n.pt.collective.contract, l10n.pt.professional.category, l10n.pt.union, l10n.pt.profession, l10n.pt.pay.grade) instead of one model per shared generic mechanism or fields.Selection for closed code tables.
    • Rejected: the generic hr.employee.attribute mechanism of hr_employee_master_data (OCA/hr) — the lists differ enough in shape (CPP is a ~2 700-entry coded classification, categories are scoped to an IRCT) that dedicated models stay simpler, and l10n_pt_hr stays independent of the generic addon.
  • Escolaridade (hr.employee.certificate) and Nível de qualificação QNQ (l10n_pt_qualification_level) are distinct fields on distinct scales; both are kept. “Nível de Habilitações Académicas” is a colloquial umbrella term covering both — no separate field.
  • Payroll-facing classifications are coded models, not Selection fields: l10n.pt.ss.regime (regimes of the Código dos Regimes Contributivos; codes are module-defined since SS publishes no numeric list) and l10n.pt.irs.withholding.table (official tables I–VII per the annual Despacho). l10n_pt_payroll maps each code to the year’s contribution rates and withholding brackets.
    • Rejected: hardcoded Selection values — payroll needs a stable coded list it can extend and join against per-year rate tables.
  • Work accident insurance (SAT) is a policy record (l10n.pt.work.insurance: insurer, policy number, coverage period) that employees reference from hr.version — several employees can share one employer policy. It matters most for independent workers, who hold their own policy; whether the employer’s master policy also lands on res.company is an open question.
  • Establishments are modelled by a dedicated l10n.pt.establishment model, not by fields on hr.work.location: the establishment is a company-level legal concept and the statutory grouping unit of both the DMR-SS file (per estabelecimento × taxa contributiva) and every Relatório Único anexo. The same record carries the SS ESTABEE code and the RU unidade-local data; hr.work.location links to it.
  • DMR-AT campo 05 “local de obtenção” is only the geographic region (Continente/Açores/Madeira/Estrangeiro), not the establishment — the worker’s region derives from the work location address.
  • The PT master-data fields live on hr.version as typed Many2one fields over the dedicated l10n.pt.* models, so they are contract-scoped and versioned with the employment data. Effective-dated history for generic attributes remains the job of hr_employee_master_data (OCA/hr); l10n_pt_hr does not depend on it.
    • Rejected: effective-dated history for these fields via hr.employee.history — decided the values are contract-scoped data that belongs to hr.version like contract type and wage.
