Portuguese localization support for Human Resources.

Features:

- Demo data for the "PT Company" (``base.demo_company_pt`` from
  ``l10n_pt``): a full set of Portuguese employees mirroring the core
  ``hr`` demo data, including a Portuguese department tree, work
  location, working schedule, contract versions and department managers,
  with Portuguese job titles.
- Extra translations (``i18n_extra/pt.po``) for the core demo Job
  Positions used by the demo data.

   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.

Notes on proposed terms:

- **Categoria profissional** is the term used on Portuguese payslips
  (recibo de vencimento) and in collective agreements. English:
  "professional category" ("job category" is also acceptable).
- **Escalão de remuneração** ("pay grade") is official for independent
  workers, whose contributions are computed on a "remuneração
  convencional" fixed in one of the pay grades (escalões) referenced to
  the IAS. It does not apply to employees on a standard contract (conta
  de outrem), whose contributions are a percentage of actual pay. Keep
  it only if the module targets independent workers.
- **Regime de Segurança Social** is the official term (seg-social.pt):
  e.g. "regime geral dos trabalhadores por conta de outrem", "regime dos
  trabalhadores independentes". English: "Social Security scheme".
- **Escalão de IRS** is official (OE tables, gov.pt): the IRS is divided
  into escalões (brackets). English: "IRS bracket" or "tax bracket". For
  payroll withholding, the operative table is the "tabela de retenção na
  fonte" (withholding table) — model the brackets behind it.
- **Nível de Habilitações Académicas**: prefer **Nível de
  qualificação**, the official term of the Quadro Nacional de
  Qualificações (QNQ, levels 1-8, aligned with EQF/ISCED). English:
  "qualification level". "Habilitações" is colloquial. Odoo CE ships
  ``hr.employee.certificate`` ("Certificate Level") translated as
  "Escolaridade" — European Portuguese users will expect the QNQ level.
- **Seguro de Acidentes de Trabalho**: official term for the mandatory
  work accident insurance; commonly abbreviated **SAT**. English: "work
  accident insurance" or "occupational accident insurance".

Statutory reporting requirements
--------------------------------

Portugal has no single annual "income declaration" from employer to
worker anymore. The recurring obligations are two monthly declarations,
both due by day 10 of the following month (Portaria n.º 33/2024 as
amended by Portaria n.º 289/2025 for the AT; the SS declaration follows
the same calendar):

- **DMR-AT** — Declaração Mensal de Remunerações to the Autoridade
  Tributária e Aduaneira (AT): employment income (category A), IRS
  withholding, mandatory social protection contributions, union dues.
- **DMR-SS** — Declaração de Remunerações to Segurança Social, I.P.:
  remuneration per worker per month split by nature code.

Related obligations: payment of withheld IRS (day 20), payment of SS
contributions (days 1-25), the annual Relatório Único to DGCP (former
GEP, roughly March-April), and the payslip content required by art.
276.º of the Código do Trabalho.

(not on the DMR): marital status / couple situation (tabela: não casado,

disability, fiscal residence (Continente, Açores, Madeira), and IRS
Jovem eligibility (age, qualification level per QNQ, years of income).

Employee master data required by the DMR-SS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The DMR-SS file has fixed-length records of 118 characters, model
``RC4008`` (normas de preenchimento, seg-social.pt). The structure is:

- ``R0`` — file header (model + version), once per file.
- ``R1`` — entity header, **one per estabelecimento × taxa
  contributiva**.
- ``R2`` — remuneration lines per worker per month of reference.
- ``R3`` — totals closing each R1 block.

"Deverão existir tantos registos (R1) quantos os diferentes
estabelecimentos e diferentes taxas que a Entidade Empregadora tenha a
declarar" — the OCC practical guide (June 2025) states the same in plain
terms: workers of each estabelecimento go in separate declarations, and
one declaration per contribution rate. So the grouping key is
**(estabelecimento, taxa contributiva)**, not the worker alone. Each R1
carries ``NISSEE`` (SS number of the employing entity), ``ESTABEE``
(4-char establishment code registered in Segurança Social Direta — SS
rejects unknown/closed codes, error DS31), ``NIF``, ``RAZSOC``,
``DATREM``; each R3 carries the same keys plus ``TAXA`` and ``TOTREGS``.
A single file may contain several R1–R3 blocks.

Master data checklist for both declarations
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- NIF (employee contact VAT), full name, date of birth, NISS — worker
  identification
- Marital status, number of dependents, disability, fiscal residence —
  IRS withholding table selection
- IBAN — salary payment and refund account
- Professional category — required on the recibo de vencimento (art.
  276.º CT) and used in SS contexts
- Contract type, schedule — payslip; work location resolves the DMR-AT
  region and the DMR-SS/RU establishment
- Social Security scheme (regime) — determines the contribution rate and
  the applicable rules
- Union membership — quotizações sindicais
- Income categorization per salary rule — maps payroll lines to the
  DMR-AT income codes and DMR-SS nature codes

Annual Relatório Único (RU) — DGCP
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Relatório Único (Portaria n.º 55/2010) is the annual social-activity
report to the DGCP (former GEP), delivered through the relatoriounico.pt
application, typically in a March-April window. It has a Rosto (employer
and establishments), Anexo A (Quadro de Pessoal — staff as of 31
October), Anexo B (entries/exits during the year), Anexo C (continuous
training), Anexo D (occupational health and safety), Anexo E (strikes)
and Anexo F (service providers). Estagiários (interns) are included in
the headcount and the Anexo A.

**Every anexo is keyed by unidade local**: Anexo A's header carries NIF
+ NISS + *número da unidade local*, and each worker row has a "Nº da
unidade local (estabelecimento)" field. Anexos B–F are likewise reported
per UL (Anexo D even asks for SST technician counts per UL). This makes
the establishment — not the department — the statutory grouping unit of
the RU, and the 31-October reference date means the worker→establishment
assignment must be reconstructible historically (``hr.version``).

Placement rules
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- ``hr.version`` holds the employee's personal data in Odoo 19
  (``ssnid``, ``marital``, ``children``, private address fields all live
  there). Fields that describe the worker or vary per contract version
  go there.
- ``hr.employee`` holds data that must survive contract changes: the
  first contractual admission date and the last promotion date.
- Per-year statutory tables (IRS brackets, IAS, minimum existence) get
  dedicated models; they are data, not employee fields.
- Open-ended statutory lists get one dedicated model each
  (``l10n.pt.collective.contract``, ``l10n.pt.professional.category``,
  ``l10n.pt.union``, ``l10n.pt.profession``, ``l10n.pt.pay.grade``)
  rather than one generic attribute mechanism or ``fields.Selection``.
  Closed code tables stay ``fields.Selection``. The module is
  self-contained: it does not depend on ``hr_employee_master_data``.
- Salary-rule mappings (income category, DMR nature codes, contribution
  base) extend ``hr.salary.rule`` and therefore land in
  ``l10n_pt_payroll``, not this module.
- Rejected: a separate ``l10n_pt.employee`` model — it would duplicate
  ``hr.version`` and break the core versioning UI.

``ssnid`` validation (NISS): implemented as a constraint on
``hr.version.ssnid``, applied only to employees of Portuguese companies
(``country_id = PT``) so other countries keep their own SSN conventions.
The NISS has 11 digits. The first digit identifies the holder type (1 =
national, 2 = foreign natural person — employees always start with one
of these). The last digit is a check digit computed over the first 10
digits with prime weights ``29, 23, 19, 17, 13, 11, 7, 5, 3, 2``:
``check = 9 − (Σ dᵢ·pᵢ mod 10)``. Demo data uses mathematically valid
numbers. This validates format only — whether a NISS is actually
assigned is confirmed only by Segurança Social.

``identification_id`` validation: the document number is stored in the
core ``hr.version.identification_id`` field. For employees of Portuguese
companies it is normalized (uppercase, no spaces) and checked against
the selected document type; with no type selected, PT employees are
treated as ``other``. ``identification_id`` of other countries is
neither normalized nor validated.

Cartão de Cidadão: the document number is 12 characters, format
``DDDDDDDD C VV D`` — 8-digit Número de Identificação Civil (NIC), its
check digit, a 2-character card version (``A–Z``, ``0–9``), and a final
check digit. Two independent checks:

1. NIC check digit (9th char): mod-11 with weights 9..2 over the 8
   digits — the same rule as the old BI and the NIF.
   ``check = resto < 2 ? 0 : 11 − resto``.
2. Final check digit (12th char): Luhn variant in base 36 over all 12
   chars, right to left, doubling every second character (``A``\ =10 …
   ``Z``\ =35, subtract 9 when the doubled value ≥ 10); the sum must be
   a multiple of 10.

Bilhete de Identidade (BI): the legacy document number is 9 digits — the
same 8-digit NIC (zero-padded) plus the same mod-11 check digit as the
CC's 9th character.

Residence permit and other document numbers have no Portuguese
check-digit scheme — format validation only (``A–Z``, ``0–9``).
Passports are not part of ``l10n_pt_id_doc_type``: they use the core
``hr.version.passport_id`` / ``passport_expiration_date`` fields and are
not validated here.

Note on work accident insurance (SAT): ``l10n.pt.work.insurance`` is a
policy record (insurer, policy number, coverage period) that employees
point at — several employees can share one policy, which fits the common
case of an employer policy covering the whole staff. For employees on a
standard contract the coverage is the employer's obligation; the
per-worker link matters for independent workers (trabalhadores
independentes), who must hold their own SAT policy. Whether the
employer's master policy also belongs on ``res.company`` is an open
question below.

Note on professional categories: each IRCT defines its own category
table, so ``l10n.pt.professional.category`` carries an optional
``irct_id``. The employee form filters the category list to the selected
IRCT plus generic categories (no IRCT).

Note on IRS withholding table selection: ``l10n_pt_irs_table_id`` is a
stored computed field implemented in ``l10n_pt_payroll`` — the employee
never picks a table directly. The annual Despacho (e.g. n.º 1179/2026,
Circular 3/2026) numbers the dependent-work tables I–VII by exactly
three determinants, all declared on the employee: family situation
(``l10n_pt_irs_family_status``), the number of IRS dependents
(``l10n_pt_irs_dependents`` — a dedicated count, since IRS dependents
can differ from dependent children), and disability of 60% or more
(``l10n_pt_disability_degree``). Tables IV–VII are the disabled variants
of I–III. Caveats: these are the mainland (continente) tables — the
Açores and Madeira have their own Despacho table sets with the same
numbering but different values, which ``l10n_pt_payroll`` must select by
region; non-resident taxpayers pay a flat rate and pensioners use tables
VIII–XI, both out of scope here; IRS Jovem is a reduction computed by
payroll (age, QNQ level, years of income), not a table determinant.

``l10n.pt.establishment`` — estabelecimento / unidade local
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Decided: a dedicated PT model, not fields on ``hr.work.location``. The
establishment is a **company-level legal concept** — it is the grouping
unit of the DMR-SS file and of every RU anexo, its SS code is registered
in Segurança Social Direta against the employing entity, and it is
needed by non-HR flows (payroll, accounting, future SAF-T/IES
obligations). ``hr.work.location`` stays the HR-level "where the worker
physically is" record.

The same record serves both authorities, but the two identifiers are
independent: Segurança Social calls it the *estabelecimento*
(``ESTABEE``, a code registered in SS Direta), the RU calls it the
*unidade local* (SGURI assigns the número). They are stored as separate
fields and are never inferred from each other; values are plain text so
leading zeros survive. If a real-world mismatch ever appears (SS
grouping ≠ physical site), separate records remain possible.

Data, not fields
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Some catalog elements need records rather than fields. These belong in
data files, not models:

- Leave types: ``hr.leave.type`` records for Férias, Baixa médica,
  Licença parental (PT-PT labels).
- Work entry types and salary rules for Horas suplementares, Subsídio de
  alimentação, Subsídio de transporte — defined by ``l10n_pt_payroll``.
- QNQ and RU code tables — Selection values and demo data.
- CPP table — ``l10n.pt.profession`` rows, loaded from the official
  CPP/2010 classification (large table, import tooling needed).
- IRCT and per-IRCT professional category tables —
  ``l10n.pt.collective.contract`` and ``l10n.pt.professional.category``
  rows per collective agreement.
- Escalões de remuneração (TI pay grades referenced to IAS) —
  ``l10n.pt.pay.grade`` rows, only if independent workers are supported.

Attribute scope — countries and companies
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Attributes are not global-on; they are scoped:

- ``hr.employee.attribute.default_country_ids`` — countries for which
  the attribute is a **default** (shown/applied unless the company turns
  it off). ``required_country_ids`` — countries for which it is
  **required**. The employee's country (company country by default)
  selects which attributes apply.
- ``hr.employee.attribute.company`` — each company keeps its own list of
  active attributes, and for each one whether it is **required** or
  **optional** for that company. This is the per-company override of the
  attribute's country defaults.

Change reasons
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

``reason`` on assignments and on the Employee Change wizard is a coded
``hr.employee.change.reason`` record (``code``, ``name``), not free
text. Each reason optionally lists the ``hr.employee.attribute``\ s it
applies to — **empty means the reason applies to any attribute** (e.g.
"Correction" fits everything, "Promotion" may only fit
``professional_category`` / ``pay_grade`` / ``irct``).

Assignment semantics
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- An assignment is valid from ``date_start`` until the next assignment's
  ``date_start`` for the same employee and attribute (``date_end``
  computed, like ``hr.version``). ``is_current`` means the period covers
  today.
- At most one current assignment per (employee, attribute) — partial
  unique constraint, same pattern as ``hr.version``'s
  ``(employee_id, date_version)`` index.
- Writing a new assignment closes the previous current period
  (``date_end`` = new ``date_start``); overlaps are rejected.
- ``reason_id`` is a coded ``hr.employee.change.reason`` (see above);
  the reason's ``attribute_ids`` constrains which attributes it may be
  used with (empty = any).
- History is reconstructible for any date, not just today — this is what
  the RU 31-October snapshot and the monthly DMR-SS grouping consume.

Employee form — Master Data page
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A "Master Data" notebook page on ``hr.employee`` lists the employee's
master data as one line per attribute:

============= ===================================
Column        Source
============= ===================================
Attribute     ``hr.employee.attribute.name``
Current value current assignment's ``value_id``
Start date    current assignment's ``date_start``
Change reason current assignment's ``reason``
============= ===================================

Only attributes with at least one assignment appear — an employee with
no union does not get an empty union line.

Row actions:

- **History** — opens the assignment list for that employee + attribute,
  all periods ordered by ``date_start``.
- **Change** — opens the Employee Change wizard prefilled with that
  attribute (single-line change).

Page action:

- **Employee Change** — opens the wizard empty, for bulk changes.

Employee Change (bulk)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The ``hr.employee.change`` wizard takes one ``date_start`` and one
``reason_id``, for the whole change, plus a list of (attribute, new
value) lines — the reason's ``attribute_ids`` must cover every attribute
in the change. Confirming creates all assignments atomically and closes
each previous current period. Typical use: a promotion that changes
professional category, pay grade and IRCT on the same date in one
recorded change.

A single-attribute change is the same wizard with one line — there is no
separate single-change model.

Relationship to ``l10n_pt_hr``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- ``l10n_pt_hr`` is self-contained: the PT lists are the dedicated
  ``l10n.pt.*`` models above, and the fields (``l10n_pt_irct_id``,
  ``l10n_pt_professional_category_id``, ``l10n_pt_union_id``,
  ``l10n_pt_profession_id``, ``l10n_pt_pay_grade_id``) are stored on
  ``hr.version``, so they are contract-scoped and versioned with the
  rest of the employment data. There is no dependency on
  ``hr_employee_master_data``.
- Coded list models (``l10n.pt.ss.regime``,
  ``l10n.pt.irs.withholding.table``) feed payroll: each record's
  ``code`` is the stable key ``l10n_pt_payroll`` maps to contribution
  rates and withholding brackets per fiscal year.
- ``hr_employee_master_data`` remains available in the OCA/hr repository
  as an optional generic mechanism (effective-dated attribute
  assignments with change history) for HR data elements that do not fit
  the dedicated PT models; installing it does not change how
  ``l10n_pt_hr`` stores data.

Open questions for ``hr_employee_master_data``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Whether assignments should also link to ``hr.version`` for attributes
  that are genuinely contract-scoped (e.g. a different IRCT per contract
  version) — v1 assumes employee-level only.
- Whether the Master Data page shows attributes with no assignments —
  the per-company ``hr.employee.attribute.company`` list decides which
  attributes are active at all; whether inactive-but-assigned or
  never-assigned attributes still appear needs a UX decision.
- Access rights: read for ``hr.group_hr_user``, write for
  ``hr.group_hr_manager`` — same split as the private-info fields.

Usage
=====

Demo employees
--------------

When the database is created with demo data enabled, the module adds a
full set of employees to the "PT Company" demo company. Switch to that
company and open Employees to see them.

Load testing
------------

To create large numbers of employees for load testing, use the
``odoo populate`` command, which duplicates the existing demo employees:

.. code:: console

   $ odoo populate -d <database> --models hr.employee --factors 100

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

**Status:** draft

Decisions
---------

- The module catalogues the HR data elements required for Portugal, with
  the Portuguese term and the recommended English term for each. The
  catalog lives in ``CONFIGURE.md``.
- Terminology follows official Portuguese sources: seg-social.pt for
  Social Security regimes and contribution brackets, OE/gov.pt for IRS
  brackets and withholding tables, QNQ/DGERT for qualification levels.
- Where Odoo CE/EE already ship a ``pt`` translation, it is reused;
  where the shipped term is a Brazilian variant (``holerite``,
  ``auxílio-transporte``, ``Licença``, ``Escolaridade``), the European
  Portuguese term is recommended instead.
- Rejected: "Nível de Habilitações Académicas" — colloquial; the
  official term is "Nível de qualificação" (QNQ).
- Rejected: "Escalão de Remuneração" for employees — it applies to
  independent workers (trabalhadores independentes) on conventional
  remuneration; standard employees contribute on actual pay.
- The recurring statutory declarations are the monthly DMR-AT (AT) and
  DMR-SS (Segurança Social), both due by day 10 of the following month.
  Their required employee master data is catalogued in ``CONFIGURE.md``.
- New fields are placed per the rules in ``CONFIGURE.md`` "Proposed
  fields": worker and contract-scoped data on ``hr.version``,
  career-history data on ``hr.employee``, per-year statutory tables in
  dedicated models, and salary-rule mappings in ``l10n_pt_payroll``.

  - Rejected: a separate ``l10n_pt.employee`` model — duplicates
    ``hr.version`` and breaks the core versioning UI.

- Open-ended statutory value lists get one dedicated model each
  (``l10n.pt.collective.contract``, ``l10n.pt.professional.category``,
  ``l10n.pt.union``, ``l10n.pt.profession``, ``l10n.pt.pay.grade``)
  instead of one model per shared generic mechanism or
  ``fields.Selection`` for closed code tables.

  - Rejected: the generic ``hr.employee.attribute`` mechanism of
    ``hr_employee_master_data`` (OCA/hr) — the lists differ enough in
    shape (CPP is a ~2 700-entry coded classification, categories are
    scoped to an IRCT) that dedicated models stay simpler, and
    ``l10n_pt_hr`` stays independent of the generic addon.

- Escolaridade (``hr.employee.certificate``) and Nível de qualificação
  QNQ (``l10n_pt_qualification_level``) are distinct fields on distinct
  scales; both are kept. "Nível de Habilitações Académicas" is a
  colloquial umbrella term covering both — no separate field.
- Payroll-facing classifications are coded models, not Selection fields:
  ``l10n.pt.ss.regime`` (regimes of the Código dos Regimes
  Contributivos; codes are module-defined since SS publishes no numeric
  list) and ``l10n.pt.irs.withholding.table`` (official tables I–VII per
  the annual Despacho). ``l10n_pt_payroll`` maps each ``code`` to the
  year's contribution rates and withholding brackets.

  - Rejected: hardcoded Selection values — payroll needs a stable coded
    list it can extend and join against per-year rate tables.

- Work accident insurance (SAT) is a policy record
  (``l10n.pt.work.insurance``: insurer, policy number, coverage period)
  that employees reference from ``hr.version`` — several employees can
  share one employer policy. It matters most for independent workers,
  who hold their own policy; whether the employer's master policy also
  lands on ``res.company`` is an open question.
- Establishments are modelled by a dedicated ``l10n.pt.establishment``
  model, not by fields on ``hr.work.location``: the establishment is a
  company-level legal concept and the statutory grouping unit of both
  the DMR-SS file (per estabelecimento × taxa contributiva) and every
  Relatório Único anexo. The same record carries the SS ``ESTABEE`` code
  and the RU unidade-local data; ``hr.work.location`` links to it.
- DMR-AT campo 05 "local de obtenção" is only the geographic region
  (Continente/Açores/Madeira/Estrangeiro), not the establishment — the
  worker's region derives from the work location address.
- The PT master-data fields live on ``hr.version`` as typed Many2one
  fields over the dedicated ``l10n.pt.*`` models, so they are
  contract-scoped and versioned with the employment data.
  Effective-dated history for generic attributes remains the job of
  ``hr_employee_master_data`` (OCA/hr); ``l10n_pt_hr`` does not depend
  on it.

  - Rejected: effective-dated history for these fields via
    ``hr.employee.history`` — decided the values are contract-scoped
    data that belongs to ``hr.version`` like contract type and wage.
